The 15-Minute Helsinki

Each 250 m cell, scored 0–5: how many of five everyday needs are within 15 minutes by the chosen mode.

How this works
  • It's a count, not an average. The number is how many of the five needs (working, supplying, caring, learning, enjoying) are reachable — never a mean. Plentiful cafés can't paper over a missing school. Five satisfied needs = a complete neighbourhood.
  • "Living" isn't scored — home is the trip origin, so we measure the five destinations you travel to.
  • Travel is cell-to-cell, not door-to-door. Each amenity is snapped to its 250 m cell, and time is a precomputed lookup between cell centres — fast for the whole region, but coarse: an amenity 14 min away in the next cell can read as just over the line.
  • Mode = a specific variant: Walk (average pace), Bike (fast cyclist), Transit (rush-hour), Car (rush-hour).
