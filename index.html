By foot, a handful of islands. By bike, a continent.

How much of everyday life sits within 15 minutes — and what these neighbourhoods lack isn’t shops, it’s care.

How this works
  • It's a count, not an average. The number is how many of the five needs (working, supplying, caring, learning, enjoying) are reachable — never a mean. Five satisfied needs = a complete neighbourhood.
  • "Living" isn't scored — home is the trip origin.
  • Travel is cell-to-cell, not door-to-door — a precomputed lookup between 250 m cell centres; fast for the whole region, but coarse.
  • Mode = a specific variant: Walk (average), Bike (fast), Transit (rush-hour), Car (rush-hour).
